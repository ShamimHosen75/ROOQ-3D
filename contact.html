Good to know

Questions we hear often

Do you stitch blouses?

Yes. Choose "stitched to size" on any saree and we will take your measurements after you order, in the atelier or over a video call.

Can I see a saree before buying?

Of course. Visit the atelier, or book a video call and we will unfold it for you, border and pallu included.

Do you ship across India and abroad?

We ship across India, and abroad on request. Delivery times and costs are confirmed with you before payment.

What is your exchange policy?

Unworn sarees with tags can be exchanged within 7 days of delivery. Stitched blouses and custom pieces cannot be exchanged.
