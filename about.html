Our story

Woven slowly,
worn for a lifetime.

ROOQ is a small atelier for sarees that are made to be kept. We work with weavers and embroiderers who still do things by hand, and we only make a few of each drape.

The atelier

A house of drapes

It started with a single trunk of sarees collected from weaving towns, and a habit of unfolding each one slowly for whoever visited. The atelier still works that way. Every drape is chosen in person, checked by hand and folded with care.

We keep the collection small on purpose. Fewer pieces means we know each weaver by name, and every saree arrives with its story.

What we hold to

Three quiet promises

01

Handloom first

Silks woven on pit and frame looms, with real zari and natural lustre. No power-loom copies.

02

Finished by hand

Zardozi, chikankari and resham work done stitch by stitch, so no two drapes are quite the same.

03

Small batches

A few pieces of each design, made without rushing. When a drape is gone, it is usually gone.

From loom to you

How a drape is made

  1. 01Sourcing

    Yarn and zari are chosen with the weaver, and colours are dyed to match the design.

  2. 02Weaving

    The drape is woven by hand, border and body together, over days or weeks.

  3. 03Finishing

    Embroidery, tassels and fall are added by hand. Every piece is checked in the atelier.

  4. 04Your fitting

    We help with blouse stitching and draping, in the atelier or over a video call.
